# Fusion, de-duplication and folding

URL normalization, RRF, folding reprints, folding Wikipedia language editions, and domain rules. Overview and rules: [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Chinese overview for people: [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Dedupe, RRF and reprints

URL-normalized dedupe and RRF fusion (`fusion.ts`): DOI (also from publisher URLs) and arXiv IDs, papers by an equal `meta.doi` (PubMed and Europe PMC link their own pages, so their URL keys differ from a `doi.org` one) or by long title + compatible year, images by image file. `foldReprints` (called by `fuse` and `mergeLateHits`) folds the same article posted on several sites into one result with `reprints`: same title (site suffix like `_CSDN博客` stripped, at least 12 characters), different sites, AND the engines' texts overlap (two-character pieces, at least half of the shorter one); it keeps the copy that is not on a republishing site (`REPRINT_HOSTS`), then the earliest date, then the best rank. Papers, images, video and notes are never folded. Which copy is the original is a guess, so the page says only "同一篇还出现在 …". Then `applyDomainRules` (blocked/boosted domains, no key needed).

## Wikipedia language editions

**Wikipedia language editions** (`wikipedia.ts`, `han.ts`, no key needed): one result per article, in the browser's language. `config.judge.foldWikipediaEditions` is optional and unset until the user chooses: `wikipediaFoldEnabled` then says on for a Chinese browser (`isChineseLanguage(Env.browserLanguage)`: `zh*`, `yue`, `wuu`, `lzh`, `nan`, `gan`, `hak`, `cdo`) and off for any other; the advanced section of the settings page shows the effective value as a switch and "恢复默认" unsets it. `runSearch` passes every list through `tidy` = `foldWikipediaEditions`, then `applyDomainRules` (partial `fused` events and late merges included; the fold is idempotent, so a late copy is merged and folded again). `parseWikipediaUrl` reads article addresses on `*.wikipedia.org`: `/wiki/X`, a variant prefix (`/zh-tw/X`, `zh-hans zh-hant zh-cn zh-sg zh-my zh-hk zh-mo`, Chinese edition only), `?variant=`, `/w/index.php?title=`, the mobile host and the old per-variant hosts. `wikipediaTarget(language)` is the edition the user reads (`zh`, with script and variant for `zh-CN`/`zh-TW`/`zh-Hant-HK`…; `zh-yue` for `yue`, `zh-classical` for `lzh`, `zh-min-nan` for `nan`; `wuu`, `gan`, `hak`, `cdo`; any other language is its own code). Two results are the same article when `wikipediaTitleKey` is equal: NFKC, lower case, Traditional turned into Simplified by `han.ts` (OpenCC `TSCharacters.txt`, Apache-2.0, 2,965 one-character pairs made by `packages/core/scripts/gen-han.mjs`; characters only, so regional vocabulary such as 軟體/软件 is not unified), symbols kept ("C++" is not "C"). In a group, copies on one edition collapse into one (the user's variant or none named, then the same script, then the best rank; the engines that returned them are merged with `absorb`); when the user's edition is in the group the other editions fold into it as `reprints` (so the page says "同一篇还出现在 zh-yue.wikipedia.org"); when it is not, they are left alone. Articles that are titled differently on two editions ("计算机" / "電腦") are not recognized: that needs Wikidata, which is an online lookup. A Chinese result addressed in the other script than the user reads (`/zh-tw/X` for a Simplified reader) is given `/wiki/X`: checked 2026-11-12 against zh.wikipedia.org that an address with no variant is served by `Accept-Language` (`zh-CN` Simplified, `zh-TW` and `zh-HK` Traditional; `en-US` got a page mixing both), so this is done only when the browser names a Chinese script. Other namespaces are plain titles (`Talk:X` folds with `Talk:X`, not with `X`). Checked 2026-11-12 with the user's Exa key (two queries, `includeDomains` the Chinese editions plus en/ja; `packages/core/test/fixtures/wikipedia-exa.json` is the 50 real addresses and titles, no page text): the real lists carry exactly the shapes above (22 of 25 addresses for 周杰伦 name a variant), 25 → 12 results for a Simplified reader with the Cantonese and Wu copies listed under the Mandarin article; and through the real extension in a throwaway headless Chromium whose profile had `intl.accept_languages` set (`~/.kiwi-search/e2e/wiki-e2e.sh`, Exa only, 10 Wikipedia results for "周杰伦 维基百科"): `zh-CN` and `zh-TW` 10 → 7 with no address left in the other script, `yue` 10 → 7 keeping `zh-yue.wikipedia.org/wiki/周杰倫`, `en-GB`/`en-US` unset 10 → 10 and switched on 10 → 8; the switch shows the default for each browser, stores nothing until clicked and then stores the opposite. Not checked: a real Google or Bing list (Exa was the only source, so how often engines return several variants of one article in a mixed list is unmeasured), Firefox, editions other than `zh`/`zh-yue`/`wuu`/`zh-classical`/`en` against real data (`simple`, `ja` and the rest only in unit tests).
