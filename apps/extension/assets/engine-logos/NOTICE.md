# Engine logos

The small marks shown after a result's site name when several engines returned it (`lib/sourceIcons.ts`, generated from this directory by `scripts/gen-source-icons.mjs`).

None of these was drawn for Kiwi Search. Each is the brand's real logo, taken from an open icon collection or from the brand's own site, and is used only to identify the engine that returned a result. Every logo belongs to its owner and none of the owners has endorsed or sponsored Kiwi Search; no permission was asked for or given. Whoever publishes a build (a store listing, say) should decide whether that is acceptable for their purpose.

Font Awesome Free Brands icons are licensed CC BY 4.0 by Fonticons, Inc. (https://fontawesome.com/license/free): the Yandex mark is from that set. SVG Logos, Simple Icons and CoreUI Brands data are CC0 1.0.
