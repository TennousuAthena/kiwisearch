Kiwi Search

Search many sources from your own browser, and get one merged, filtered list with an AI overview and citations.

Install the extension Open search

Opening the search needs the extension installed: the extension does the searching in your browser, the website only provides the page.

What it does

  • Many search modes. Web, images, video, news, shopping, academic, and social and community platforms.
  • Many sources side by side. Search engines, video sites, scholarly indexes and APIs, queried together. Each one can be switched on or off in the settings.
  • Merged, not stacked. Duplicate links and reposts collapse into one result, and the results several sources agree on come first.
  • Less junk. A decision model hides content farms and off-topic pages. You can also subscribe to block lists.
  • AI overview with citations. Use your own OpenAI-compatible model, or your browser's built-in one.
  • Works with your agents. A local MCP server lets any MCP-compatible agent run the same searches through your browser.

Your browser, your data

Every result comes from your own browser, your own logins and your own API keys. Kiwi runs no accounts, proxies or servers, and results are shown only to you. See Privacy.
