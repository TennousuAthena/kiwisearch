Privacy

This is the privacy statement for the Kiwi Search extension and the website kiwi.moe.

In one sentence

Searching happens in your own browser. Kiwi runs no accounts, proxies or back-end servers, so no “Kiwi server” receives your searches.

The extension

  • Your search goes to the sources you have switched on. A source rendered by the browser (search engines, video sites, community platforms) is opened by your own browser, with your IP address and your logins in that browser; an API source uses the key you entered. The sources that are on by default, and a switch for each, are in the extension's settings; you can turn any of them off.
  • AI features are used only once configured. When you enter an API key and turn on the summary or the decision model, your search, the titles, addresses and snippets of results, and the page text used for the summary are sent to the service you configured. The decision model's preset service is TypeSafe; nothing is sent while no key is entered. With the browser's built-in model the content is processed on your device.
  • Site icons are requested by your browser directly from the site a result is on (its /favicon.ico and its home page), not through a third-party icon service. A site you have never visited therefore receives one icon request.
  • Block-list subscriptions are off by default. When you turn one on, the extension downloads the list from the third-party host you chose.
  • Kept on your device: your settings, the API keys you entered, source status and the icon cache, in the browser's extension storage. They are not uploaded. The extension does not write searches or results to storage; they stay in the open page and are gone when it is closed.
  • The MCP server listens only on 127.0.0.1 on your machine, and the extension connects to it only after you have paired a token.
  • The extension has no analytics, advertising or telemetry, and sends nothing to any server of Kiwi's.

The website kiwi.moe

  • The website is static pages: no back end, no cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts. The search page (the home page) carries a content security policy that forbids any network connection.
  • Your search is written after the # in the address. A browser does not send what follows the # when it requests a page, so the search does not reach the website's server.
  • The hosting provider sees, as for any website, the visitor's IP address, the time, the browser identifier and the requested path.
  • No address on the website takes a search: it only ever appears after the #.
  • Without the extension, the website does not search for you. It shows links to other search engines, and your search goes to an engine only when you click its link. You can have the page remember one choice on your device (in the browser's localStorage, not uploaded).
  • With “the extension's own page” chosen in the extension's settings, a search does not contact kiwi.moe, and the website cannot use the extension.

Your choices

You can turn off any source, leave every AI service unconfigured and choose the extension's own page. Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored in your browser.

Contact and changes

Questions and feedback go to GitHub. This page is updated when the statement changes.
