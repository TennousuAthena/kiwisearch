Kiwi Search is a browser extension that turns the new tab into a search page. Supported browsers are listed in the [install guide](docs/USAGE.md#安装).

**Your browser, your data.** Every result comes from your own browser, your own logins and your own API keys. Kiwi runs no accounts, proxies or servers, and results are shown only to you.

## Features

- **Many search modes.** Web, images, video, news, shopping, academic, and social and community platforms.
- **Many sources side by side.** Search engines, video sites, scholarly indexes and APIs, queried together. Each one can be switched on or off in the extension's settings, which always show the full list.
- **Merged, not stacked.** Duplicate links and reposts collapse into one result, and rank fusion puts the results several sources agree on first.
- **Less junk.** A decision model hides content farms and off-topic pages. You can also subscribe to block lists in uBlock Origin, uBlacklist or hosts format, with no key needed.
- **AI overview with citations.** Use your own OpenAI-compatible model, or your browser's built-in one when it has one. Overviews stream in with `[n]` citations in the language you choose, and short factual questions get a quick answer above the results.
- **Built for research.** Smart academic search checks each suggested paper against a scholarly index. News can be limited to the last day, week or month. Shopping shows product cards from online stores.
- **Works with your agents.** A local MCP server lets any MCP-compatible agent run the same searches through your browser.

## Notice

Kiwi Search runs only in your own browser, and only when you or your own agent start a search. It does not solve captchas for you, and each source is queued and rate-limited. Search engines' terms usually forbid automated queries, and these measures do not mean they have authorized it. Use it for personal purposes, at your own risk, and follow local law and each source's terms. Third-party logos and product names belong to their owners.
